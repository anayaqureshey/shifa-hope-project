Heritage & Action

Shifa & Soil is a social enterprise founded by pre-medical students dedicated to bridging health disparities. By crafting heritage-inspired goods, we generate self-sustaining capital to deliver hands-on medical supplies, diagnostic tools, and community health kits to underserved areas worldwide.

100% Funded to Relief
Direct Field Deployment

Our Operational Model

Connecting traditional craftsmanship directly with sustainable public health outreach.

01

Artisan Crafting

We design and handcraft small-batch botanical goods, traditional embroidery pieces, and tote bags using sustainable materials.

02

Resource Allocation

100% of store proceeds are pooled into purchasing vital clinical supplies, blood pressure monitors, and hygiene kits.

03

Hands-On Deployment

Our founding team travels directly to partner clinics and underserved global communities to deliver resources and run health literacy workshops.

Artisan Goods

Every item is handmade by our founding team. All net profits fund health screening kits for field trips.

Founders & Vision

Combining faith-inspired values, heritage artistic traditions, and pre-med clinical aspirations.

Pre-Medical & Public Health Core

As undergraduate pre-medical students, we recognize that health equity requires active engagement outside classroom walls. This project allows us to gain hands-on operational leadership while addressing preventive health needs in underserved areas.

Heritage & Faith-Inspired Service

Grounded in Islamic traditions of stewardship (Amanah) and continuous charity (Sadaqah Jariyah), we fuse Pakistani artisan crafts with actionable global health advocacy.
